General Required Documents

For Salaried Individuals:
- Identity Proof (Aadhaar, PAN)
- Address Proof (Utility bill, Aadhaar, Passport)
- Income Proof: Latest 3 months payslips, Form 16 for last 2 years.
- Bank Statements: Last 6 months salary account statement.

For Self-Employed Individuals:
- Identity/Address Proof
- Income Proof: Last 2 years IT Returns with computation of income, P&L, and Balance Sheet certified by a CA.
- Bank Statements: Last 6 months current account and savings account statements.

Product Specific Additional Documents:
Home Loan: Property title documents, agreement to sell, approved building plan, valuation report (if available).
Education Loan: Admission letter from institute, detailed fee structure.
Personal Loan: No additional documents usually required beyond standard KYC and income proof.
